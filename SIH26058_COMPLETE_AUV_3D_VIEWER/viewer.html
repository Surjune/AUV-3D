Frequency plan electrical vs acoustic

The adaptation engine sets f0, bandwidth, pulse length and drive level from the water, then routes each ping to the band whose projector resonance is nearest f0. Projector spans are drawn approximately; the usable spans come from impedance measurement.

Low-power design

  • Timer + DMA stream each ping from a RAM buffer; a sine lookup table replaces run-time trigonometry, so the CPU works only when the water changes.
  • Only the selected band's PA pair is enabled for a ping (PD_L / PD_H); both pairs power down between pings.
  • The PA load switch and the 30 V boost have their own enables (LOAD_SW_EN, BOOST_EN) for long idle periods.
  • The INA241A measures 30 V rail current per ping. Power figures are to be measured on the prototype.

Adaptive software-defined sonar transmitter payload

An STM32G474 adapts the waveform (band, frequency, bandwidth, pulse, PRI and drive level) to depth, temperature and host-provided water parameters (potentiometer dials on the ADC in the bench demo), and drives two acoustic bands from one DAC. Only the selected band is enabled for each ping.
